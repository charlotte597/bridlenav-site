The part most apps skip

We tell you which routes have actually been ridden.

A route drawn from a map and a route someone has ridden on a horse are not the same thing, and you deserve to know which one you’re about to load a horse for. Every route in BridleNav carries a badge.

13

Verified

Personally ridden and checked by BridleNav or a trusted local guide.

9

Documented

Based on a local rider’s detailed guide, but not yet personally ridden by the BridleNav team.

20

Map-plotted

Drawn from maps, not yet ridden. Full written guide, parking and notes — but no spoken turn-by-turn yet.

1

Map guided

Spoken turn-by-turn built from map data rather than a rider’s own description. The line follows mapped bridleways and the directions name real tracks and gates — but none of it has been checked on the ground.

5

Shared by a rider

Recorded and written up by another rider, and read by us before it went live. Not ridden by the BridleNav team, and we say so on the route.

Forty-eight routes so far — forty-three of ours and five shared by riders — every one badged honestly. If nobody has ridden it yet, we say so.
