Delete your account

Last updated 4 October 2026

BridleNav is published by BridleNav Limited. If you have a BridleNav account and you want it gone, you can do it yourself in about ten seconds, and you do not have to ask us or give a reason.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open BridleNav on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Your account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when you are asked. That is it — it happens straight away.

Or ask us to do it

If you have lost access to the phone you signed in on, or you would simply rather we did it, email hello@bridlenav.com from the address you signed up with and say you want your account deleted. We will do it and write back to confirm, within one month at the very latest and usually the same week.

What gets deleted

When your account goes, so does everything attached to it:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name, if you set one
  • Any rides you had synced to your account

All of it is removed from our systems. It is not archived, and we cannot get it back for you afterwards, so do export anything you want to keep first.

One thing is not attached to your account, so it is not deleted with it: if you ever used “Report a problem” to tell us about a gate or a wrong turn, that report stays, because it is how we keep the route right for everyone else. It is tied to your phone rather than to you, and carries no name or email. If you would like yours removed too, just say so in the same email and we will.

What stays on your phone

Some things never reach us at all — your emergency contact, your chosen voice, your app settings, and any rides that were never synced. Deleting your account does not touch those, because they are on your phone and not ours. Deleting the app removes them.

What we have to keep, and for how long

If you ever paid for a subscription, UK tax law requires us to keep the record of that sale for six years after the subscription ends. That record is held by our accountants as part of our books. It is kept because the law says we must, not because we want it, and it is not used for anything else.

Your payment itself was taken by Apple or Google, never by us — we have never held your card details, so there are none for us to delete. If you want your subscription cancelled as well, that is done in your App Store or Google Play account, not here.

Emails you have sent us are kept for up to two years after your question is dealt with, then deleted.

You do not need an account at all

Worth saying: BridleNav works without one. Turn-by-turn navigation runs on your phone whether or not we know who you are. An account only exists so your rides can follow you between phones, and so you can subscribe.

Any questions

Email hello@bridlenav.com and a person will answer. Our full Privacy Policy sets out everything we hold and why.
